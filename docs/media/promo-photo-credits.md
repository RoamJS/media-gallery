## Changes made in the videos

- Every photo is **scaled down**. Gallery thumbnails and note thumbnails are **cropped** by the app's own `object-fit: cover` (10:7 cards in the gallery). Previews show the whole image at its original proportions. Nothing else is changed: no colour change, retouching, compositing or overlay on the picture itself, except the app's own similarity badge drawn over a card's corner in the semantic-search beat.
- The file names (`road-trip.jpg` and so on), the note text, the page names and block names are **fictional demo labels**. They are not the photographers' titles, they don't describe who uploaded anything, and the photographers are not depicted as users or authors of the notes.
